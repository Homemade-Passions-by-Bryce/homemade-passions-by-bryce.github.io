A familiar face.
A personal touch.

Homemade Passions is the work of owner and pastry chef Bryce Cline. An Auburn native, Bryce built her bakery around custom orders and local markets before opening a Riverton storefront in 2024.

Today, that same personal approach is at the heart of the shop—from a treat for your morning to something made for your biggest moments.
